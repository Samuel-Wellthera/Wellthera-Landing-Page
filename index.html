Clear answers before you book.

Is my treatment covered by insurance?

Coverage depends on your individual plan, provider, practitioner type, annual limits, and any referral requirements. Wellthera does not have access to the full details of your insurance policy, so we recommend confirming your coverage directly with your provider.

Do you offer direct billing?

Yes — we offer direct billing for eligible services through major providers including Sun Life, Canada Life, and Manulife, where your plan allows it.

Do I need a doctor’s referral?

Most of our services do not require a referral. Some insurance plans, however, may request one for reimbursement — please check with your provider.

Can I choose my practitioner?

Absolutely. You’re welcome to book with the practitioner of your choice, subject to availability.

Is parking available?

Yes, complimentary on-site parking is available for all patients at our Big Bay Point Road location.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment so we can offer the time to another patient.
